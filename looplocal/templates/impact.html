How we count

Waste kept in use is the sum of owner-estimated weights for items marked repaired. Cancelled, open and unrepairable items are excluded.

Potential emissions avoided = repaired weight × an illustrative category factor. This assumes a repair displaces a replacement; it is not a measured carbon saving or a lifecycle assessment.

Factors (kg CO₂e per kg): Electronics 18 · Appliances 12 · Bicycles 6 · Furniture 3 · Textiles 8 · Other 4.

These are explicitly hypothetical demonstration factors, not UN-endorsed values. Replace them with locally validated lifecycle data before making environmental claims. Seeded records are demonstration data.
